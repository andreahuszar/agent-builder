## Invoice ID Patterns

Mock invoices use these ID prefixes:
- `needs-info-*` - Missing critical data (11 invoices)
- `blocked-*` - Matching issues or exceptions (13 invoices)
- `mock-*` - Overdue invoices (6 invoices)
- `due-*` - Due soon invoices (7 invoices)
- `cn-*` - Credit notes (2 invoices)
- `pf-*` - Pro forma invoices (2 invoices)
- `approval-*` - In approval (4 PO invoices)
- `approval-nonpo-*` - In approval (3 Non-PO invoices)
- `archived-*` - Archived invoices (1 invoice)
